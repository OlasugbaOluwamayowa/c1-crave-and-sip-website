Ask us on WhatsApp ↗
How do I place an order?+

Add your drinks to the bag, complete the checkout details and choose “Send Order to WhatsApp”. Your message will be prepared for you. Check it and press Send in WhatsApp.

Are these menu prices final?+

Not yet. The products and prices on this development version are examples only. The Crave & Sip team must confirm the final menu before the website goes live.

How much is delivery?+

Delivery charges depend on the business's confirmed delivery arrangements. The team will confirm the fee and final amount before accepting your order.

Can I order for an event or office?+

Absolutely. Use the group-order button and share your event type, date and estimated quantity. The team can then confirm options, pricing and delivery arrangements.

When will my drinks arrive?+

Your delivery time will be confirmed during the ordering conversation. Sending a WhatsApp request does not automatically confirm availability or delivery.

Which payment methods do you accept?+

Payment methods still need to be confirmed by Crave & Sip. Please ask the team during your order conversation.
