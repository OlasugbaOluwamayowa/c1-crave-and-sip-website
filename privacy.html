Privacy notice.

Draft — must be reviewed and approved by Crave & Sip before launch.

This website starter does not include analytics, account creation, a payment gateway, or a server-side database. The shopping cart is stored in your browser's local storage on this device. Checkout details are used in the browser to prepare a WhatsApp message; this starter does not send those details to a Crave & Sip server.

When you choose to continue to WhatsApp, the website opens a WhatsApp click-to-chat link with your order details prefilled. WhatsApp is a separate service and its own privacy terms apply. Your message is not sent until you press Send in WhatsApp.

Product photography in this starter is loaded from Unsplash URLs, and web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Those third-party services may process technical request data when your browser loads their assets.

Before publication, Crave & Sip should confirm its actual data practices, contact details for privacy enquiries, retention arrangements for WhatsApp orders, any analytics or hosting logs, and any applicable legal requirements.
